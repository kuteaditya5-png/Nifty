NEW DISPLAY
The separate 5m Breakout Setup card shows bullish/bearish direction, source
(ORB / PRIOR6), signal time in IST, lifecycle status, entry, stop and targets.
The chart uses the very same server values: ENTRY, STOP, T1 and T2, all labelled
as NIFTY spot. OR HIGH / OR LOW stay dashed blue. No rejection text or arrows.
Select the 5m chart to see the setup. Other intervals clear these setup lines.

LEVEL RULES
Use the latest confirmed completed-candle breakout of the displayed session.
Entry = signal candle close (reference only, not an executed/fill price).
Stop = signal low minus breakout buffer for UP; high plus buffer for DOWN.
Risk R = entry-to-stop distance. Targets = 1R and 2R in the signal direction.
These are geometric projections, not forecasts of achievable prices or option
premiums. They do not promise 35 premium points or override CE/PE/WAIT gates.

SETUP TRACKING
Track the six subsequent completed 5m candles (30 minutes after signal close).
Mark target/stop touches, expiry and session close. If stop and a target are
touched in the same candle, mark AMBIGUOUS; OHLC cannot establish touch order.
New confirmed signals replace older setups. Completed historical setups can
retain their lines with an explicit non-active status on the card.
Flagged data, gaps and stale active data suppress target lines. Chart refresh
clears old targets before fetching new data so failed requests do not retain them.
Touches are based only on completed candles, not live ticks.

No volume sourcing or prediction/trade gates were changed.
